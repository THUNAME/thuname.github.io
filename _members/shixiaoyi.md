I am currently a 3rd-year Ph.D. candidate at the [Institute of Network Science and Cyberspace](https://www.insc.tsinghua.edu.cn/) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Ying Liu](https://www.insc.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1157/2456.htm). I received my bachelor's degree from the [Department of Automation](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University, in 2024. My current research interests include **programmable networks** and **large language models**.
